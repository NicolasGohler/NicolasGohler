## Nicolas Goehler

GTM engineer and founder of [Releasi](https://www.releasi.co). I build go-to-market infrastructure: collectors, evidence stores and review systems that turn public records into buying signals a sales team can act on.

### What I build

- **Signal collectors** across company registers, regulatory filings, contract awards, permits, accelerator cohorts and job boards. 80+ public sources so far.
- **Evidence-first data models** that keep the dated source on every record and track discovery, qualification and contact readiness separately.
- **Delivery layers**: hosted review dashboards, CRM-ready records and webhooks.

Stack: Python, SQLite, Claude Code, Apollo, Vercel.

### Open source

**[gtm-public-signals](https://github.com/NicolasGohler/gtm-public-signals)**
GTM engineering infrastructure for public data. Collectors that turn SEC Form D, Companies House, UK contract award and NPPES records into dated sales signals. Standard-library Python, with CSV, JSONL or webhook output.
